How it works

1 · ResolveEvery ticker is mapped to its bStocks, Ondo and xStocks contracts on BSC from Binance's tokenized-securities registry.
2 · Quote & normalizeEach issuer is quoted for the same USDT amount via baw market-order quote, then divided by its live share multiplier so prices are compared per real share.
3 · ShieldEach fill is checked against the US last price (or the oracle median when the US price is missing). Anything more than 300 bps above reference is flagged toxic and never routed.
4 · ExecuteThe cheapest safe route is executed with baw market-order swap and only reported as done after the order reaches FINISHED.
Quotes are live on BSC mainnet. Off-hours liquidity is thin, so prices shift between scans. Not financial advice.
